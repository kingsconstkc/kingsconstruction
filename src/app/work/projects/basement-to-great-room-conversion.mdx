## Overview

This basement did its job holding boxes and a furnace, and nothing else. The homeowners wanted square footage they'd actually use: a hangout space for the kids, a bar for game nights, and a clean, bright finish that doesn't read like a basement.

## Scope

- Framed a defined great room with a separate utility/storage closet
- Wet bar with cabinetry, counter seating, and a kegerator run
- Media wall with concealed low-voltage wiring and floating shelving
- Egress-compliant window work and a refreshed stairwell
- Full electrical, data drops, and HVAC zoning for the lower level
- Moisture mitigation: sealed walls, new vapor barrier, and a dehumidifier line

## What We Did

Basements punish you for skipping the unglamorous work, so we started with the envelope — moisture testing, sealing, and a vapor strategy before a single stud went up. Then we reframed the ceiling grid to get as much head height as the joists allowed and ran all wiring and plumbing for the bar while the walls were open.

The media wall carries a custom feature treatment, and every outlet, shelf, and fixture was laid out to a plan rather than eyeballed on site.

## The Result

The family's most-used room in the house — and a finished space that could pass for a main floor. Game nights upgraded, storage moved to a closet that actually fits it, and a basement that finally earns its square footage.
