## Overview

Not every project needs a demo dumpster. This one was a detail package: the homeowners had clean, open rooms that just felt *unfinished*. We gave the house a design language — bold feature walls, crisp trim, and hardware choices — and carried it from the entryway through every living space.

## Scope

- Custom geometric feature walls with metallic gold inlay strips
- Full trim upgrade: doors, casing, baseboards, and window surrounds
- Recessed and accent lighting designed around the feature walls
- Coordinated hardware and finish package throughout the home
- Paint and finish schedule matched to the home's navy-and-gold palette

## What We Did

Feature walls fail when the layout is improvised, so we mocked up the panel geometry on paper first and transferred it to the wall with laser layout — every reveal equal, every inlay line dead straight. The trim package followed the same rule: consistent profiles, tight scribe lines, and caulk lines you don't notice because there's nothing to notice.

Lighting was tuned to graze the feature walls instead of flattening them, so the gold inlay reads warm by night and sharp by day.

## The Result

A house that photographs like a design magazine spread and lives like a normal home. The feature walls became the backdrop for every room, and the trim work is the kind of detail guests notice without being able to name.
