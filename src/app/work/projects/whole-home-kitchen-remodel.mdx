## Overview

This kitchen had good bones but a bad layout: cramped pantry, builder-grade cabinets, and a wall that cut the room off from the rest of the house. The homeowners wanted a space that works for weeknight cooking *and* weekend gatherings — with modern finishes that still feel at home in a classic Kansas City house.

## Scope

- Removed the partition wall between the kitchen and dining area
- Full custom cabinetry with a walk-in pantry rework
- Quartz countertops and full-height tile backsplash
- New island with seating, prep sink, and pendant lighting
- Upgraded electrical, recessed lighting, and HVAC return
- Refinished and tied in the hardwood flooring

## What We Did

Demo and structural work came first, with framing opened up to create one continuous living space. We built the cabinetry layout around the family's routine — coffee station by the door, drop zone off the garage entry, and a work triangle that keeps two cooks out of each other's way.

Plumbing and electrical were relocated to support the island, and every finish choice was made with real life in mind: scrub-friendly paint, durable LVP-to-hardwood transitions, and a backsplash that wipes clean in one pass.

## The Result

A kitchen that runs from the front door to the back deck without a single wall in the way, and a noticeable jump in how the whole main floor feels. The homeowners host more, cook more, and — most importantly for us — haven't called back about a single punch-list item.
